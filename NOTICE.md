## 1. Healthcare Transparency Notice (Not Medical or Legal Advice)
CareCheck is an informational healthcare transparency tool designed to help consumers understand hospital itemized charges, statutory compliance under the No Surprises Act, and standard charges. It is not medical or legal advice.

## 2. Consumer Privacy Defaults & Air-Gap Invariant
By default, medical bills and chargemasters are processed 100% locally in-browser with zero remote network transmission. No PHI or HIPAA-covered records leave the device.

## 3. Software Bill of Materials (SBOM)
A complete, machine-readable CycloneDX Software Bill of Materials (`bom.json`) is generated on build in CI via OWASP `@cyclonedx/cdxgen` and attached to release artifacts.
